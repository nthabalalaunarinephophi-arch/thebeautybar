The beauty bar is a retail store that was build to provide customers with high-quality beauty and cosmetic products at affordable prices. This store was found by Chantell Moon in March 2012. She started this business because she was one of the the girls, who cannot afford products for herself back in her days. She wanted to help young girls to be able afford products in affordable prices. The store offers a wide range of items, including skincare products, makeup, hair care products, perfumes, and beauty accessories.

At The Beauty Bar, we believe beauty is for every body Our mission is to bring you high-quality beauty products, that make you look good,feel confident, and live beautifully every day.
